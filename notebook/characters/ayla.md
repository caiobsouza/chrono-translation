# Ayla

- Speaker labels in the script: `Ayla`
- Home: 65,000,000 BC, Ioka
- Source: [Chrono Compendium](https://www.chronocompendium.com/Term/Ayla.html), summarized in our own words

## Who they are

- Chief of Ioka in prehistory, chosen for her strength. Has no magic because she predates the Frozen Flame.
- Kino, her tribesman, steals the Gate Key out of jealousy. She gives Crono the Dreamstone needed to forge the Masamune and later joins the party.
- Lives by the law of the strong. Married Kino after the story.

## Voice in the English script

- Primitive grammar, third person for herself: `Me Ayla. Ayla like strong people.`, `Ayla go home now.`, `Kino no come.`
- Very short lines (5 words), exclamations in 72 percent. Calls Crono `[crononick]`, a code the game fills in.

## English versus original

- The wiki notes that some exchanges about Ayla (with Lucca and Marle) were censored in the American release. They are not in this ROM, so do not restore them.
