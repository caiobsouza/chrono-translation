# Dalton

- Speaker labels in the script: `DALTON`
- Home: 12,000 BC, Zeal
- Source: [Chrono Compendium](https://www.chronocompendium.com/Term/Dalton.html), summarized in our own words

## Who they are

- Ambitious, egotistical commander of Zeal's security forces and aide to Queen Zeal. Cruel to underlings, loves taunting, and is often the butt of the joke.
- Put in charge of the Ocean Palace construction, edged out by the Prophet, then guards the Blackbird. Uses Golems in his boss fights.

## Voice in the English script

- Boastful and taunting, exclamations in 83 percent: `The Aero-Dalton Imperial!`, `My poor Golem! That was inexcusable!`. Fake grandeur that collapses.
