# Queen Leene

- Speaker labels in the script: `LEENE`, `QUEEN`
- Home: 600 AD, Guardia Castle
- Source: [Chrono Compendium](https://www.chronocompendium.com/Term/Queen_Leene.html), summarized in our own words

## Who they are

- Kind ruler of Guardia in 600 AD, kidnapped by Yakra and rescued by Crono, Lucca and Frog. She leaves a letter for her descendant Marle.
- IMPORTANT: the label `QUEEN` in the script is shared by Queen Leene and Queen Zeal (and possibly Queen Aliza). The block era comment says which. The Zeal queen is imperious, Leene is gentle.

## Voice in the English script
