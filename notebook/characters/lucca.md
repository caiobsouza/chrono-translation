# Lucca

- Speaker labels in the script: `Lucca`
- Home: 1000 AD, Truce
- Source: [Chrono Compendium](https://www.chronocompendium.com/Term/Lucca.html), summarized in our own words

## Who they are

- Genius inventor, Taban's and Lara's daughter. Her Super Dimension Warp starts the adventure.
- Her mother lost the use of her legs in a machine accident in 990 AD. That is why she studies science, and she later gets a chance to prevent it.
- Fights with guns and Fire magic. Repairs Robo and drives much of the technical plot.

## Voice in the English script

- Longer lines than Marle (about 6.4 words), exclamations in 56 percent. Confident, analytic, a little bossy.
- Uses capitals for stress (`YOU get her back!`). Leans on `I guess`, `Of course`, `So this is...`.
