# Enemies and bosses without their own file

Short notes for bosses and monsters that carry no dialogue label of their own. Source: [Chrono Compendium](https://www.chronocompendium.com/Term/Characters_(Chrono_Trigger).html), paraphrased. Names of bosses are proper nouns, keep them unchanged in dialogue and check the glossary for the versions used in the monster list.

| Name | Who | Notes for translation |
|---|---|---|
| Frog King | Stole the Hero's Medal, appears only in Frog's flashback. | Keep the name. |
| Giga Gaia | Beast that guards Mt. Woe for Zeal. | Keep the name. |
| Golem | Dalton's construct, appears in several boss fights and flees from heights. | The Japanese version portrayed it as effeminate to sell the fear. The English does not, do not add it. |
| Heckran | Beast in Heckran Cave, controls a few Mystics in 1000 AD, warns Crono that Magus summoned Lavos. | Keep the name. |
| Sir Krawlie | Weak boss of the Sewer Access. Speaks under the label `SIR KRAWLIE` (4 lines). | Boasting knight who is actually weak, comic contrast. |
| Lavos Spawn | Lavos's offspring in Death Peak and the Black Omen. | Keep the name `Lavos`. |
| R Series | Robo's robot `brothers`, reprogrammed by Mother Brain to scrap him. | Keep `R Series`, consistent with the label `RX-XR`. |
| Retinite | Mystic creation under Fiona's villa that causes desertification. | Keep the name. |
| Slithers (Slitherta and Slitherpon) | Yakra's two henchmen guarding the vault. | Called `DUMB` and `DUMBER` in the US version, a joke pair. Translate them as a matching joke pair and reuse both names everywhere. |
| Son of Sun | Guardian of the Sun Stone. | Keep the name. |
| Zombor | Undead boss in the Cathedral, upper and lower halves. | Keep the name. |
