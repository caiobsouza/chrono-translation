# Robo

- Speaker labels in the script: `Robos`
- Home: 2300 AD, Proto Dome
- Source: [Chrono Compendium](https://www.chronocompendium.com/Term/Robo.html), summarized in our own words

## Who they are

- R-66Y, a robot reactivated by Lucca. Struggles with his purpose after being labelled useless by other robots.
- The script token is `Robos`, not `Robo`. It exists so words starting with `Robo` do not break when the player renames him.
- Stays behind for 400 years to help Fiona's forest and first suggests that a hidden Entity guides the party.

## Voice in the English script

- Formal and precise: `Greetings. I am Robo.`, `Lucca, I will miss you.`, openers like `So!`. Exclamations in only 39 percent of lines, the lowest of the party.

Proposals, confirm before relying on them.

## English versus original

- The wiki notes that the claim that Robo has no emotions is an English-version addition, not in the Japanese original. The English lines are stiff by design.
- Decision for you: keep the stiff English tone (matches the ROM), or allow a warmer voice.
