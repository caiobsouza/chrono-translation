# Crono's Mom

- Speaker labels in the script: `MOM`
- Home: 1000 AD, Crono's house
- Source: [Chrono Compendium](https://www.chronocompendium.com/Term/Crono%27s_Mom.html), summarized in our own words

## Who they are

- Crono's only parent. Gives him his allowance and greets each new party member with a joke line.
- Ends up going through a Gate by accident at the Moonlight Parade.

## Voice in the English script

- Warm and scatterbrained, exclamations in 84 percent. Calls people `dear`: `Come on, sleepy head! Get up!`.
- The greeting jokes for each new party member are some of the most quoted lines. Keep the comic timing.
