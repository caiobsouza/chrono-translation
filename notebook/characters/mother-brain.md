# Mother Brain

- Speaker labels in the script: none (no dialogue label)
- Home: 2300 AD, Geno Dome

## Who they are

- AI that ran the Geno Dome and created Robo and Atropos XR. Corrupted after Lavos's eruption, she plans a nation of machines and tries to convince Robo he was made to study and eliminate humans.

## English versus original

- The wiki says her final mission was garbled in the English translation. It suggests she wanted to stop Lavos from sending seeds to other planets, while the Japanese says the planet will recover and a robot nation should inherit it. When translating her speech, check the Retranslation for the intended meaning.
