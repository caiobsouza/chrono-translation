# Ozzie

- Speaker labels in the script: `OZZIE`
- Home: 600 AD, Magus's Lair and Ozzie's Fort
- Source: [Chrono Compendium](https://www.chronocompendium.com/Term/Ozzie.html), summarized in our own words

## Who they are

- Commander of the Mystics. Found the young Janus after the Ocean Palace incident and raised him into a war leader.
- Sets traps in Magus's Lair and later holes up in his own fort. His descendant Ozzie VIII appears in modern Porre, see `minor-npcs.md`.

## Voice in the English script

- Blustering and gloating, exclamations in 78 percent, short lines (5.4 words): `Grrrr! You're through!`, `Oh, and you again!`. Comic villain, the butt of his own traps.
