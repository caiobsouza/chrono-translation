# Magus

- Speaker labels in the script: `Magus`, `JANUS`, `PROPHET`
- Home: 12,000 BC Zeal and 600 AD
- Source: [Chrono Compendium](https://www.chronocompendium.com/Term/Magus.html), summarized in our own words

## Who they are

- Born Janus, prince of Zeal and Schala's younger brother. Thrown into the Middle Ages by the Ocean Palace incident, where Ozzie finds him.
- Becomes the leader of the Mystics with one goal, to destroy Lavos. Kills Cyrus and curses Glenn.
- Returns to Zeal disguised as the Prophet, tries to kill Lavos and fails, then joins the party.
- Three labels, one person. `Magus` is the adult in 600 AD and later, `JANUS` is the child in Zeal, `PROPHET` is the disguise.

## Voice in the English script

- Ellipses in 59 percent of his lines, the highest of anyone. Cold, sarcastic, controlled: `Rather drab, isn't it?`, `Harumph!`, `Hmph! Idiots...`.
- Janus is a petulant child (`What a filthy hovel.`). The Prophet is smooth and courtly (`Your majesty... They are the evildoers I warned you of.`).

## Proposed pt-BR voice

Proposals, confirm before relying on them.

- Dry and controlled, short sentences, keep his ellipses. No slang, few exclamation marks.
- Janus: shorter and prouder, a child's disdain. Prophet: formal and polished.
- Keep the token `Magus`. Treat `Janus` and `Prophet` as different registers of the same person.

## Related terms

- Ozzie, Slash, Flea, Alfador (his cat), Schala, Queen Zeal, Mystics, Masamune, North Cape
