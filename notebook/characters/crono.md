# Crono

- Speaker labels in the script: `Crono`
- Home: 1000 AD, Truce
- Source: [Chrono Compendium](https://www.chronocompendium.com/Term/Crono.html), summarized in our own words

## Who they are

- Silent teenage protagonist from Truce, raised by his mother. Fights with a sword and Lightning magic.
- Meets Marle at the Millennial Fair and sets the story moving by going after her when the warp sends her to 600 AD.
- Dies facing Lavos in the Ocean Palace and is replaced by a clone doll until the party revives him with the Chrono Trigger.
- The ending implies he and Marle end up together.

## Voice in the English script

- He has 2 spoken lines in the whole script, both in the ending slide show. His replies are menu choices.
- Other characters talk about and to him constantly, so his name appears everywhere. Never inflect or decorate it.
