# Yakra and Yakra XIII

- Speaker labels in the script: none (no dialogue label)
- Home: 600 AD and 1000 AD, Guardia
- Source: [Chrono Compendium](https://www.chronocompendium.com/Term/Yakra.html), summarized in our own words

## Who they are

- Monsters that impersonate the Chancellor in each era. The Compendium redirects their bio to the Chancellor, see `chancellor.md`.
- Yakra (600 AD) kidnaps Queen Leene. Yakra XIII (1000 AD) frames the king over the Rainbow Shell and orders Crono's execution.
- The Slithers, his two henchmen, are called `DUMB` and `DUMBER` in the US version (`Slitherta` and `Slitherpon` in Japan).

- Speaks as the Chancellor until the disguise drops, so the lines are under the `CHANCELLOR` label.
