# Frog

- Speaker labels in the script: `Frog`, `GLENN`
- Home: 600 AD, Guardia
- Source: [Chrono Compendium](https://www.chronocompendium.com/Term/Frog.html), summarized in our own words

## Who they are

- Glenn, squire to the knight captain Cyrus, turned into a frog by Magus after Cyrus is killed. Lives in the Cursed Woods and serves Queen Leene.
- Carries guilt over Cyrus for most of the game. The Masamune is the sword he guards.
- Label `GLENN` in the script is the same person before the transformation.

## Voice in the English script

- Archaic and knightly. Markers in the English: `'Tis`, `hath`, `thee`, `nary a soul`, `-eth` endings, `I await thee`.
- Ellipses in 37 percent of his lines. Short and grave, and formal even when angry.

## Proposed pt-BR voice

Proposals, confirm before relying on them.

- Archaic vocabulary in Portuguese (`deveras`, `outrora`, `mui`, `ó`, `cavaleiro`), full sentences, no slang.
- Address form decided: `você`, never `tu` or `vós` forms. Apply it to every Frog, Glenn and Cyrus line.
- `GLENN` lines (the young squire) use the same formal register but plainer and more earnest.

## English versus original

- Ted Woolsey's English makes Frog a formal medieval knight. In the Japanese original he was rougher, more direct and did not speak in archaic patterns. The wiki quotes an example where he insults Ayla bluntly.
- The English script is what the ROM contains. Following it keeps the game consistent, restoring the Japanese tone is a separate decision.
