# Marle

- Speaker labels in the script: `Marle`
- Home: 1000 AD, Guardia Castle
- Source: [Chrono Compendium](https://www.chronocompendium.com/Term/Marle.html), summarized in our own words

## Who they are

- Princess Nadia of Guardia, who sneaks out of the castle disguised as a commoner. The script uses both `Marle` and `Nadia`.
- Cheerful and impulsive, she is the group's morale. Her pendant reacts to the warp and sends her to 600 AD, where she is mistaken for Queen Leene.
- Her relationship with her father, King Guardia XXXIII, is the emotional thread of the 1000 AD castle scenes.

## Voice in the English script

- Shortest and most excitable of the party: about 6 words per line, `!` in 62 percent of her lines, questions in 30 percent.
- Casual and playful. Uses the game's `«...»` marks for sarcastic emphasis (`I didn't «pick up» anything!`).

## Proposed pt-BR voice

Proposals, confirm before relying on them.

- Informal spoken Brazilian: `pra`, `né`, `tá` are fine. Keep exclamations and the playful tone.
- Keep `Nadia` as it is in the source. Do not merge it with `Marle`.
