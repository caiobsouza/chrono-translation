# Flea

- Speaker labels in the script: `FLEA`
- Home: 600 AD, Magus's Lair and Ozzie's Fort
- Source: [Chrono Compendium](https://www.chronocompendium.com/Term/Flea.html), summarized in our own words

## Who they are

- Magician in the Mystic army and comrade of Ozzie and Slash. Presents as a woman (padded vest) and insists on it, yet shouts `I'm a GUY!` when insulted.
- Taunts Frog about Cyrus and later calls Magus a traitor. The wiki says he may be transgender.
