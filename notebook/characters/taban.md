# Taban

- Speaker labels in the script: `TABAN`
- Home: 1000 AD, Lucca's house
- Source: [Chrono Compendium](https://www.chronocompendium.com/Term/Taban.html), summarized in our own words

## Who they are

- Lucca's father, inventor and blacksmith, descendant of Banta. He and Lucca built the Telepod. Makes the Taban Suit and Taban Vest.

## Voice in the English script

- Enthusiastic tinkerer, exclamations in 74 percent: `Take a look Lucca! I borrowed a bit of the Sun Stone to create something nifty!`.
