# Spekkio

- Speaker labels in the script: `SPEKKIO`
- Home: End of Time
- Source: [Chrono Compendium](https://www.chronocompendium.com/Term/Spekkio.html), summarized in our own words

## Who they are

- A shape-shifting `God of War` at the End of Time who teaches magic and can be fought. His form depends on the viewer's strength.

## Voice in the English script

- Playful show-off, exclamations in 70 percent: `I AM the Master of War! Whew! Sometimes I scare myself!`. Teases each new party member.

## Proposed pt-BR voice

Proposals, confirm before relying on them.

## English versus original

- The wiki says the Japanese Spekkio was more cynical and openly called Gaspar an old codger. The English one is jovial. Decision for you: follow the English tone (matches the ROM) or sharpen it.
