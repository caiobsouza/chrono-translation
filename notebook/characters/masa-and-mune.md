# Masa and Mune

- Speaker labels in the script: `MASA`, `MUNE`
- Home: 12,000 BC to 600 AD, Denadoro Mts / Masamune
- Source: [Chrono Compendium](https://www.chronocompendium.com/Term/Masa_%26_Mune.html), summarized in our own words

## Who they are

- The mischievous twin spirits of the sword Masamune, said to be Melchior's dreams. Mune wants to be the wind.
- They test challengers, then travel with Frog's sword.

## Voice in the English script

- Teasing and playful, they finish each other's lines. Mune: `Whooosh!`, `Humans are so...silly!`.

Proposals, confirm before relying on them.
