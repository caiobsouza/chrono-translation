# Chancellor

- Speaker labels in the script: `CHANCELLOR`, `REAL CHANCELLOR`
- Home: 600 AD and 1000 AD, Guardia
- Source: [Chrono Compendium](https://www.chronocompendium.com/Term/Chancellor.html), summarized in our own words

## Who they are

- The king's right hand. There are two, one per era, and Yakra impersonates the real one in both.
- The impostor in 1000 AD is the more malevolent one and orders Crono's execution.

## Voice in the English script

- Officious and pompous, exclamations in 65 percent: `Princess Nadia! Your friends, I presume?`.
- The script uses the same label for the real chancellor and the impostor. The era comment in the block tells them apart.

## Proposed pt-BR voice

Proposals, confirm before relying on them.

- Formal and courtly, slightly pompous. The impostor can be a touch more cutting, but keep it in the same voice.
