# Minor and unnamed NPCs

One line each. These characters have little or no dialogue of their own, or share generic labels such as `ELDER` or `GIRL`. Source: [Chrono Compendium](https://www.chronocompendium.com/Term/Characters_(Chrono_Trigger).html), paraphrased.

## Named

- **Alfador**: Janus's cat. Only likes Janus, follows Magus. Keep the name.
- **Banta**: metalworker in the Middle Ages who makes Leene's Bell, ancestor of Taban. Wants a scientific female descendant, a joke that pays off with Lucca.
- **Bandeau**: builds the Medina to Truce ferry. Keep the name.
- **Catalack, GI Jogger, Green Ambler, Steel Runner**: Millennial Fair race participants. Their names are jokes or descriptions, check the glossary and the race text.
- **Dactyl**: winged reptiles Ayla summons to reach the Tyrano Lair. Keep as the same word in the item and tech lists.
- **Doreen**: sister of Masa and Mune, lives in Enhasa and asks the party philosophical questions about reality (`Am I a butterfly dreaming I'm a man...`). Keep a poetic tone.
- **Gato**: singing combat training robot at the Fair, sings a memorable ditty. Rhyme and meter of the song matter, translate it as a song.
- **Ozzie VIII**: descendant of Ozzie who bosses others around in modern Porre. See `ozzie.md`.
- **Queen Aliza**: Marle's late mother. Her last words move King Guardia XXXIII. See `king-guardia-xxxiii.md`.
- **Toma XIII**: Toma's modern descendant in Choras, tells Crono about combining the Rainbow Shell and Sun Stone. See `toma.md`.
- **Piette, Vicks, Wedge**: soldiers in Norstein Bekkler's show. Their names are Star Wars references (Piett, Biggs, Wedge). Keep them unchanged.

## Unnamed or background

- **Arris Dome Man**: a man who died looking for food in the lower Arris Dome. Only known through remains and a found Seed.
- **Bullfrogs**: two comic frogs who explain the Sewer Access switches and appear later in the Black Omen scene. Keep them a comic pair.
- **Carpenter of Choras**: one person per era (Middle Ages and present), lost tools, repairs for fees.
- **Cat**: appears in several eras, mostly triggering comic traps. Crono's house has one.
- **Chef's Descendant**: fiery cook in 1000 AD. See `chef.md`.
- **Earthbound Chief**: humble, caring leader of the Earthbound, in contact with Schala.
- **King Zeal**: died before the story. No lines.
- **Laruba Chief**: old man of Laruba who prefers hiding to fighting. Ayla's famous retort says he is dead on the inside, keep its sting.
- **Piano Man**: a Kilwala who plays music at the Snail Stop in Porre.
- **Porre Mayor and Porre Elder's Wife**: the greedy mayor refuses to give the Sun Stone. A free piece of Jerky changes the wife's descendants toward charity.
- **Sewer Access Man**: left a journal page in the sewer. Its text is a page of a diary in a frightened voice.
- **Trann Dome Man**: carries a Seed across Lab 16 from the Arris Dome.
- **Truce Dome Director**: seen only in the game over ending, at the controls until rocks fall on him.
- **Zeal Plant Woman**: asks the party whether to burn a Seed on Queen Zeal's order. The question and answer options are worded carefully, translate both consistently.
- **Gobb**: appears as a house name, `Gobb's house`, in the places list. No character notes.
