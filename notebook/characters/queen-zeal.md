# Queen Zeal

- Speaker labels in the script: `QUEEN`
- Home: 12,000 BC, Zeal Palace and the Black Omen
- Source: [Chrono Compendium](https://www.chronocompendium.com/Term/Queen_Zeal.html), summarized in our own words

## Who they are

- Queen of Zeal, mother of Schala and Janus. Once kind, she becomes obsessed with Lavos's power after the king dies.
- Commissions the Ocean Palace, causing the incident, and later rules the Black Omen. Fought twice.
- The label `QUEEN` is shared with Queen Leene and possibly Queen Aliza. See `queen-leene.md` for samples and use the block's era comment to tell them apart.
- King Zeal (her husband) has no lines. He died shortly before the story.

## Voice in the English script

- Imperious and commanding: `Show your manners!`, `Schala, raise the power of the Mammon Machine to its limit!`.
