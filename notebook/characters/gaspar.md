# Gaspar

- Speaker labels in the script: `GASPAR`, `OLD MAN`
- Home: 12,000 BC Zeal, End of Time
- Source: [Chrono Compendium](https://www.chronocompendium.com/Term/Gaspar.html), summarized in our own words

## Who they are

- The Guru of Time and the party's guide. Lives at the End of Time under a lamp post, and builds the Chrono Trigger.
- Wry and tired rather than solemn.
- IMPORTANT: at the End of Time his lines are labelled `OLD MAN`, not `GASPAR`. `OLD MAN` is a generic label used by 52 lines of many elders, so the era comment (`end of time (gaspar's stories, Spekkio etc)`) is what identifies him.

## Voice in the English script

- Tired and reminiscent with dry humor. Lots of ellipses in the `OLD MAN` lines (50 percent).

## Proposed pt-BR voice

Proposals, confirm before relying on them.

## English versus original

- The wiki says the line `One of you is close to someone who needs help` originally meant that talking to the party members at the End of Time reveals what to do in each home era. It is not a riddle. Keep it neutral and hint-like.

## Related terms

- Guru of Time, End of Time, Spekkio, Epoch, Clone, Chrono Trigger
